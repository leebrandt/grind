# The Facts

Tracy Lee Brandt
Born in Spokane, WA(I think) on January 4th, 1948 (I'm sure) to Gilbert and Cricket Brandt. I know he was an airplane mechanic in the Navy. Got out and went to work at Warner Robbins Airforce Base the 70s. Got a job in Ridgecrest, CA in 1980 at the China Lake Naval Weapons Center. Took early retirement in 1990s sometime and moved to Gardner/Minden, NV. Worked as a slot mechanic for a few years. Had a stroke in 2007 and ended up on disability. Lived with me in 2007/8-ish, before moving to Topeka, KS for a while. THen going back out to where most of his family is, in the Reno, NV/Portola, CA area.


From Gloria:
Well, your dad was petty much a loner. I remember him having only one good friend in high school.People liked him a lot..he was very good at math. I remember, in high school, he would get phone calls from classmates saying that they didn't understand a problem. And he would walk them through it. He even know what page # the problem was on!! He dropped out of college (from boredom) and joined the Navy when he was 19, I think. So most of our siblings don't remember too much about him because they were pretty young.
Oh! I remember he made a pair of stilts and he would walk all over the neighborhood on those things! He loved them.
And learning to ride a bicycle, mom forgot to tell him how took the brakes! 🤣 Rode right into the raspberry patch before he crash! In those days you pedaled backwards to stop.
And he loved the Red Skelton show.
Thiught of 1 more thing...when he was working and helping my mom with food and bills, he would give his whole paycheck and keep only $10 for gas so he could get back and forth to work and sometimes he and I would have to leave school at lunch time and rush home to maybe put a roast in the oven so the oven for dinner that night cuz mom was at the hospital all day with my dad after his industrial accident.

From Mom:
Don't know much, other than what u have already mentioned, about his childhood.  We met in LA.
