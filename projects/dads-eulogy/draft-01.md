# Tracy Lee Brandt Sr.

My dad (Tracy Lee Brandt Sr.) died last Tuesday. He'd been sick for a while, so it wasn't a shock or anything. But it's still a bit jarring to get that kind of news. At first, I didn't even understand what my aunt was saying. Oh! You mean, he *passed* this morning... A million images of my father ran through my head. Him showing me how to ride a bike. Taking me hunting, fishing, and camping. Teaching me that a man protects and supports his family and that, even though Dad *definitely* gets the big piece of chicken, we save it for him because he eats last.

As a kid, Tracy was good at math and often helped the other kids with their math homework. When *his* father got hurt, he got a job to help with the bills AND finished high-school, lettering in track. He joined the Navy and was stationed in San Diego as an airplane mechanic. He met a young girl in LA, who later became his wife and the mother of one pain-in-the-ass son. After the Navy, he worked as an aviation mechanic as a civilian. He earned his Associate's Degree in Electronics Engineering and ended up as a computer network administrator.

When I was growing up, he always had the broke-down cars and the worn-out clothes. Always making sure his family was taken care of first, then making do with what was left without complaining. I am eternally grateful for every bit of himself that he shared with me. I am who I am because of who he was. Rest easy, Dad.

> Here's to you and here's to me. If we should ever disagree: fuck you, here's to me. ~One of the many inappropriate things he taught me.
