# Well, the Linux Box Shit the bed. And it may be my fault.

I had big plans for this Geekend(tm). I was gonna build out an AI/Docker playground on my home lab. A proper little cluster to learn some new tricks, run some models, maybe actually get around to that container orchestration stuff I've been putting off. My Big Machine was gonna be the star of the show.

Then Friday morning happened.

In the morning, the Linux box had freaked out a bit. Windows weren't closing, I was having to force-quit some processes. So I restarted. Which made it worse.

I crashed-out (as the kids would say).

I was ready to swear off Linux forever. Throw every computer in this house (the ones I own anyway) in the burn barrel out back. Screaming at no one, "No *wonder* Linux can't win on the desktop. Just random failures out of nowhere. *Screw* open source!"

Turns out the drive was overheating.

I ran some diagnostics — actually, an AI helped me figure it out. The CPU temp had been rising, and the filesystem had booted in read-only mode. BTRFS had failed on boot and the system locked things down to protect itself.

Once I knew *what* was happening, I started digging into *why*.

The heat had caused the nvme drive to fail on a sector. 

The dock was generating heat, and the mini-PC was sitting right below the shelf, its intake fan sucking up all that hot exhaust, right in its face. When I went to bed, I left the computer on and running hot all night.

I'd never had that happen before. Spent ALL of Saturday morning trying to figure out why my main computer wasn't working, cursing the open-source Gods the whole time.

Now my Geekend(tm) is gonna be spent rebuilding what I lost. And reorganizing my desk.
