# Shouldn't my hobbies be at least *as* important as my work?

The fact that we push out hobbies to the side when our lives (kids, etc.) intrude is bad enough, but we do it for *work*, too. We take time away from our families to do work stuff, should we have at least one hobby that is *at least* as important as work?

* It's part of our mental health
* It encourages our family members to do the same


Just an idea.
