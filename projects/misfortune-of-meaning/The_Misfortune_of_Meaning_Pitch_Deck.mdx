# 🎬 Logline

> When a forgettable author’s book accidentally becomes a viral literary sensation, the criminal enterprise secretly laundering money through his fake publishing contract is forced to juggle police investigations, cartel pressure, and the rising fame of a man who has no idea he’s in the middle of a high-stakes game.

---

# 🎭 Tone & Style

- **Genre:** Crime Comedy / Ensemble Heist / Satire  
- **Tone:** Irreverent, sharp, absurd, grounded in emotional truth  
- **Vibes:** _Ocean's Eleven_ × _Rat Race_ × _The Pink Panther_  
- **Visual style:** Slick indie crime with moments of cartoonish escalation

---

# 🌍 The Premise

**Page Turners** is a small, indie publisher that’s actually a front for a high-functioning fencing and laundering operation.

They publish terrible books by mediocre writers no one reads—including **Charles Honeycutt**, a detective novelist who thinks his slow trickle of royalty checks are from real fans.

When Charles' latest book—_**The Misfortune of Meaning**_—accidentally catches fire with the elite art crowd, and then goes viral thanks to a YouTuber, the crew must scramble to contain the growing attention.

**Their goal:** keep the cartel satisfied, the police distracted, the author clueless… and their asses out of prison.

# 🧑‍🤝‍🧑 Main Characters

## Charles Honeycutt
- Mid-40s. Sweet, balding, meek.
- Writes mediocre noir fiction.
- Believes he’s finally “being discovered.”
- Oblivious to the criminal chaos swirling around him.

## Queenie Turner
- 27. Mixed-race. Sharp, composed, tough.
- Runs Page Turners and the entire fencing op.
- Wants out of the game. Just needs one last big score.
- Treated Charles as a disposable asset—until he became a liability.

## Eddie Turner
- 23. Queenie’s younger brother.
- Tech whiz, lady chaser, side-hustle addict.
- Loyal to Queenie, but doesn’t want out.

## Reecey Turner
- 21. Queenie’s younger sister.
- Bookish, beautiful, and deeply observant.
- Studying psychology. Unofficial lie detector.
- Likes Robbie, but uninterested in romance.

## Robbie Dupree
- 18. Large, simple, loyal.
- Bad home life. Treated like family by Queenie growing up.
- In love with Reecey.
- Dies protecting her during the gala heist.

## Liam "Lee" McHenry (real name: Darren McElroy)
- 40s. Black Irish. Undercover cop.
- Loud, annoying, always trying to renegotiate his cut.
- Gets shot during the climax—believed dead.
- Reveal: He survives. Retires to desk duty to be with his kids.

## Lt. Harper
- Lead investigator. Quiet, methodical.
- Begins unraveling the laundering trail.
- One step behind—until she isn’t.

# 📚 Plot Overview

## ACT I – The Front
- Page Turners operates quietly, laundering stolen goods.
- Charles writes for them. He thinks he's living modestly off sales.
- Queenie keeps everything tight—until the new book stirs buzz.

## ACT II – The Spark
- _The Misfortune of Meaning_ goes viral in niche art circles.
- A YouTuber fuels its popularity.
- Sales become real. Attention grows.

## ACT III – The Inferno
- Cartel demands more.
- Police investigation heats up.
- Liam (UC cop) stirs trouble inside the operation.
- Queenie plans one last job to escape.

## ACT IV – The Gala
- Literary gala. Charles is the guest of honor.
- Queenie sets the heist in motion.
- Liam is shot. Robbie dies saving Reecey.
- Chaos erupts. Harper moves in.

## ACT V – The Deal
- Queenie offers intel on cartel pipelines in exchange for immunity.
- The crew enters Witness Protection.

## ACT VI – The Rewrite
- Charles publishes _The Honeycutt Affair_, based on “true events.”
- Becomes a best-seller.
- Liam (Darren) is alive. Retired. Watching his kids sleep.

# 🧠 Why Now?

- A smart, satirical jab at influencer culture, scam culture, and the weird intersection of art, crime, and media attention.
- Ensemble-led stories with heart and absurdity are hot (_The Gentlemen_, _Only Murders in the Building_).
- Shines a light on the invisible people behind “overnight success”—and the ones caught in the crossfire.
