# Introducing Myself

For those who don't mind a short novella, why the hell would you need an introduction to *me*? You don't. You could close the email and go back to your life. But I'm writing this for three reasons:

* I feel like a lot of misunderstanding happens when people don't know at least a *little* about each other. Particularly when working remote and communicating via Slack.
* I'm secretly hoping you'll do the same thing and send me an email back introducing *yourself*. Could be just, "Hi, I'm Dave Matthews. I'm a dev on the core team. I like back-end better than front-end, and I like playing with my yorkie, Jimbo!" Or whatever your story is.
* I love to write. As anyone on the team, who has received so much as a Slack message from me. I'm really trying to be less "novel-like", but it's a work in progress.

So, with the dumb stuff out of the way, here goes mine:

I've been a developer (professionally) since 1997 (yes they had computers in the '1900s', shut up). Started in ASP classic (with VBScript) and Coldfusion, and ended up in Node and React. I've used plenty of Docker, but no K8s or Terraform/OpenTofu. I've deployed to AWS at most of my jobs, but never did any DevOps stuff.

If you ever hear me say any version of: "I've been working in this industry for thirty years", know that it's not ego. It's imposter syndrome. When one someone mentions a technology I really *should* know, that fear creeps up and whispers in my ear, "Tell 'em how long you've been doing this. Then they won't think you're a dumb-ass!" Feel free to make fun of me. "Surely not! No one was *alive* back then!", I deserve it.

I spent the last few years as a Developer Advocate and dev manager. Every step took me farther and farther away from regular software development, and I was getting burned out. So I saved up, quit Pluralsight and tried a *bunch* of stuff. I also started coding again. First, working with Claude to "vibe-code" a few things. They can pontificate and do press tours about "the state of AI", but they don't know.
